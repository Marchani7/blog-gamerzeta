## Start with a stable baseline

Before chasing ultra settings, let the game run consistently. Update your graphics driver, close launchers you do not need, and use a frame-rate cap that your monitor can hold most of the time.

## Tune the settings in the right order

Set resolution and display mode first. Then adjust shadows, volumetric effects, reflections, and post-processing. These options usually cost more performance than texture quality, so keep textures higher when your GPU has enough memory.

## Keep the first session useful

Write down what changed after each setting pass. That small habit makes troubleshooting easier and gives you a repeatable profile for future patches or hardware changes.
