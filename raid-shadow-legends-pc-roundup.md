## Match events to your account

The best event is the one your current roster can complete efficiently. Check the required resources before committing, especially when several events overlap.

## Treat offers as optional

Compare any bundle with the time and resources it actually saves. A clear limit keeps an exciting event from turning into an expensive routine.

## The takeaway

Use the PC client for a calm review of your roster and inventory. The larger view makes it easier to decide what matters before the next reset.
