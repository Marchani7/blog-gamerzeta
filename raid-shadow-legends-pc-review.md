## Team building is the hook

The best part of Raid is comparing roles and building a team that solves a specific problem. The decisions feel meaningful when you focus on one milestone at a time.

## The grind and the PC client

The PC client gives the game a comfortable home, especially for inventory cleanup and planning. It cannot remove the repetition, though, so the healthiest approach is to set a short session goal.

## Who should play it

Players who enjoy collection systems and steady optimization will find depth here. Anyone sensitive to aggressive offers or repeated farming should approach with clear limits.
