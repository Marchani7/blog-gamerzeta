## The strategy loop

Fleet Command is built for gradual decisions. The appeal comes from choosing what to improve next and watching several small choices combine into a stronger position.

## PC experience and value

The PC format makes fleet management easier to scan, especially when multiple timers and missions compete for attention. Value depends on treating purchases as optional accelerators rather than requirements.

## Who should play it

It suits patient strategy fans who like a persistent map and a daily routine. It is a weaker match for players looking for quick sessions with immediate resolution.
