## Choose a routine before chasing everything

The early game gets noisy when every mission looks urgent. Pick one main progression goal, then use daily tasks and side activities to support it instead of splitting resources across every upgrade.

## Spend resources where they unlock options

Prioritize improvements that open new missions, increase flexibility, or reduce repeat work. A small efficiency gain repeated every day is often worth more than a flashy upgrade you rarely use.

## Make PC play comfortable

Use a stable window layout, readable scaling, and a short daily checklist. Strategy games reward attention over speed, so a calm interface is a real advantage.
