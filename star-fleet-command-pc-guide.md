Star Trek Fleet Command presents many timers, currencies, missions, and upgrade paths at once. The best early strategy is to build one dependable progression loop instead of spending resources everywhere.

## Upgrade your station with a purpose

Operations controls your overall progression and unlocks more buildings and systems, but rushing it can leave research and support buildings behind. Before increasing Operations, complete the important research and building requirements that strengthen your current ships and economy.

Keep construction and research active whenever possible. These two queues create steady account growth even when you only have time for a short session.

## Build your second ship early

The official Academy explains that the Orion Corvette can be unlocked through the second node of the combat research tree. Its blueprints can also appear from level 7–11 hostile chests. A second ship gives you more flexibility for missions, mining, and combat tasks.

Do not treat every ship as a combat ship. Survey ships are designed for mining, while Explorers, Interceptors, and Battleships follow a combat triangle: Interceptors pressure Battleships, Battleships pressure Explorers, and Explorers pressure Interceptors.

## Officers matter as much as ship power

Place an officer in the captain position to activate the Captain’s Maneuver. Bridge officers use their officer abilities, and officers from compatible groups can add synergy. Build crews around a specific task—hostiles, mining, or survival—instead of choosing only the highest visible power number.

## A simple daily route

1. Collect completed building and research upgrades.
2. Start the next useful timer.
3. Check daily goals and current events.
4. Complete missions that unlock systems or required resources.
5. Use the correct ship and crew for mining or hostile targets.
6. Recall ships and protect the station before leaving.

## Common beginner mistakes

Avoid upgrading every ship, spending premium currency to finish short timers, and ignoring research. Bookmark useful systems so you do not repeatedly search the galaxy map. Once station combat becomes available, understand shields and station protection before holding large exposed resources.

## Gamerzeta recommendation

Focus on one Operations milestone, one combat ship, one survey ship, and research that supports your current goal. A narrow plan produces faster practical progress than reacting to every notification.
