## Presentation and control

NTE makes a confident first impression. On PC, the larger display gives its environments room to breathe, while a keyboard-and-mouse setup keeps movement and camera control direct.

## Performance and progression

The experience is strongest when exploration and combat move together. It is less convincing when menus or progression steps interrupt that flow. A few settings passes are worth making before judging performance.

## Who should play it

Players who enjoy discovering a new world and tuning a PC profile will find a solid starting point. Those expecting a fully settled live-service structure should wait for more updates.
