## Start with airflow and dust

Clear intake and exhaust paths make every other adjustment easier. Clean filters, remove cable blockages, and keep the case away from a wall or enclosed shelf.

## Tune the fan curve gradually

Use a moderate curve that responds to sustained temperature rather than every short spike. Test a game for at least ten minutes before making another change.

## Reduce noise at the desk

Distance, placement, and soft surfaces can matter as much as a component swap. Keep the PC ventilated, then place the loudest source farther from your ears.
